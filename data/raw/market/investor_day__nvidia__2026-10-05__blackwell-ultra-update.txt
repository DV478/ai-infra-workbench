Nvidia today provided an updated product roadmap at its 2026 Investor Day. Key highlights:

- Blackwell B200 is in full production, with Q3 2026 shipments of approximately 420,000 units across hyperscaler and neocloud customers.
- Blackwell Ultra B300 is on track for general availability in Q2 2027, with initial sampling to lead customers beginning January 2027.
